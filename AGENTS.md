## Design & Typography Guidelines
- **No Unsolicited Eyebrow / Pill Badge Subheadings**: Do NOT add floating pill badges, category tags, or artificial eyebrow subheadings (e.g. `THE RESERVE COLLECTION`, `✦ THE ART OF...`) above hero titles or section headings. Keep headings clean, confident, and direct.
- **Editorial Poise**: Avoid generic SaaS / AI-generated tropes (e.g., floating icon pills, three-column generic feature bullets with icons, chunky dark boxes with white cards inside). Use genuine editorial spacing, authentic serif typography (e.g. Baskerville Italic), and minimal hairline dividers.
