2. Step-by-Step Client Meeting Walkthrough Script
Meeting Script
Meeting Flow (What to say & show)
  • Step 1: Welcome & Homepage (2 mins): Share screen on /. Show Hero banner, luxury navbar, shop by category tiles (Nuts, Dried Fruits, Seeds, Gourmet, Combos, Gifting), co-founder story, and customer review badges.
  • Step 2: Category & Product Details (3 mins): Navigate to a category page. Open a product detail page (/menu/cashews). Show package weight selection (250g/500g/1kg) and demonstrate dynamic price updates.
  • Step 3: Cart Drawer & Checkout (2 mins): Click "Add to Bag", open the slide-out Cart Drawer, show quantity controls and the Free Shipping progress bar. Click "Proceed to Checkout" to demo address inputs and payment choices (Razorpay/COD).
  • Step 4: Admin Panel Showcase (3 mins): Switch tab to Admin Panel. Show the Dashboard sales overview, demonstrate adding/editing a product with Cloudinary image upload, and show updating order status (*Pending → Shipped → Delivered*).
  • Step 5: Feature Proposal & Automations (5 mins): Open the Visvam_Client_Feature_Proposal.pdf. Walk through subscriber welcome emails, WhatsApp order alerts, abandoned cart recovery, and discount promo code capabilities.
3. Point-by-Point Questions to Ask the Client
Questionnaire
1. Design & Branding
  • Do you approve the luxury color scheme (Warm Cream & Dark Forest Green) and typography?
  • Are all product category names aligned with your current inventory list?
  • Do you have additional banner images or founder story details to update?
2. Payments & Shipping
  • Which active payment gateway account do you have (Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe, Paytm)?
  • Do you want to enable Cash on Delivery (COD)? Should we set a COD fee (e.g. ₹50)?
  • Which logistics partner will you use (Shiprocket, Delhivery, NimbusPost)?
3. Marketing, Email & WhatsApp Automations
  • Welcome Email Discount: Should subscriber signup give 10% OFF (`WELCOME10`) or flat ₹100 OFF?
  • WhatsApp Integration: Should we enable automated WhatsApp order status alerts & post-delivery review requests?
  • Abandoned Cart Recovery: Should we send automated reminder emails 1h & 24h after cart drop-off?
  • Gift Packaging: Would you like to offer custom gift box packaging (+₹99) at checkout?
