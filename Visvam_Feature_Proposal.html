1. Automated Email & Newsletter System
A. Subscriber Newsletter & Welcome Email
Essential
When a customer enters their email in the website footer or popup, an automated welcome email is sent instantly.
Your Preference / Decision:
  • Send Welcome Email with 10% Discount Code (e.g., WELCOME10)
  • Send Welcome Email with Flat Discount (e.g., ₹100 Off on 1st Order)
  • Send Brand Welcome Email without discount
B. Transactional Email Notifications
Essential
Automated emails sent to customers during their order journey to build trust and cut down customer service inquiries.
Automated Triggers Included:
  • Order Placed: Instant itemized receipt & order confirmation.
  • Order Dispatched: Shipping details & live courier tracking link.
  • Order Delivered: Delivery confirmation & thank-you note.
C. Advanced Email Automation Options
High Revenue Impact
Smart email sequences that automatically recover lost sales and re-engage interested buyers.
Select Automations to Enable:
  • Abandoned Cart Recovery: Automated email sent 1 hour & 24 hours after a customer leaves items in their bag (recovers 15–25% of lost carts).
  • Back-in-Stock Alerts: Automatically notifies waiting customers when an out-of-stock product is restocked.

Growth Tool
