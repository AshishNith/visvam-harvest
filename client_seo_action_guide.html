Technical SEO Summary & Action Overview

The technical SEO infrastructure has been fully built into the visvam.in codebase (including XML sitemaps, robots.txt, JSON-LD product & store schemas, canonical tags, and OpenGraph parameters). To complete live search indexing, traffic tracking, and commercial Google Shopping listings, please follow the 5 step-by-step setup guides below.

1. Google Search Console (GSC) Domain Setup Action Required
Purpose & Business Value

Allows Google to index all 20+ dry fruit product pages, monitor site search performance, track keyword rankings in India, and report search queries bringing visitors to visvam.in.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in with the main Viśvam Google Account.
  2. Click Add Property from the top-left dropdown.
  3. Select the Domain option and enter visvam.in (or select URL prefix and enter https://visvam.in).
  4. Choose the HTML Tag or TXT Record verification method.
  5. Copy the verification code (e.g. google-site-verification=XYZ...) and share it with the technical team.
  6. Once verified, click on Sitemaps in the left menu.
  7. Enter https://visvam.in/sitemap.xml in the "Add a new sitemap" box and click Submit.
✔ Deliverable to Tech Team: Google Site Verification Token
2. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Property & Measurement ID Action Required
Purpose & Business Value

Tracks live visitor counts, traffic sources (Google search, social media, direct), user engagement, popular products, and customer checkout conversions in real-time.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Go to https://analytics.google.com/ and sign in.
  2. Click Admin (Gear icon) at the bottom left ➔ Click Create Account.
  3. Name the account Viśvam, then create a property named Viśvam (select currency: Indian Rupee INR).
  4. Under Data Streams, choose Web.
  5. Enter Website URL: https://visvam.in and Stream Name: Viśvam Web.
  6. Click Create Stream.
  7. Copy the Measurement ID displayed at the top right (format: G-XXXXXXXXXX).
✔ Deliverable to Tech Team: GA4 Measurement ID (e.g. G-ABC123XYZ)
3. Google Merchant Center (Free Shopping Listings in India) Recommended for E-Commerce
Purpose & Business Value

Displays Viśvam's dry fruits, jumbo almonds, W240 cashews, and gift boxes directly inside Google Search results and the Google Shopping tab across India for free.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Go to https://merchants.google.com/ and sign up with your Google account.
  2. Enter your business name: Viśvam and country: India.
  3. Under Business Info, verify and claim the website domain https://visvam.in (automatic if using the same account as Search Console).
  4. Set up shipping rates (e.g. Free shipping over ₹999) and store address details.
  5. Navigate to Products ➔ Feeds to upload your product catalog or connect via Google Content API.

  3. Target platform: Select Web. Container Name: Viśvam Web.

✔ Deliverable to Tech Team: GTM Container ID (e.g. GTM-WXYZ123)
