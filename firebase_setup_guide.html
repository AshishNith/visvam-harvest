Firebase Authentication Setup & Activation Guide
Target Project: visvam-1be38
Feature: Phone OTP & Passwordless Auth
Date: August 20, 2026
1 Enable Phone Authentication (SMS OTP)
Required
Enables 6-digit SMS OTP verification for instant phone login across India and internationally.
  1. Log in to the Firebase Console at https://console.firebase.google.com.
  2. Open your project: visvam-1be38.
  3. In the left sidebar under Build, click Authentication.
  4. Click on the Sign-in method tab.
  5. Click on Phone (or Add new provider > Phone).
  6. Toggle the Enable switch to ON.
  7. (Recommended for testing) Expand Phone numbers for testing:
    Add phone +91 9999999999 with test OTP 123456 to test without spending SMS quota.
  8. Click Save.
2 Enable Email Link (Passwordless Magic Link)
Required
Enables 1-click passwordless login via email magic links sent directly to customer inboxes.
  1. In Authentication > Sign-in method, click Email/Password.
  2. Ensure the first toggle Email/Password is switched ON.
  3. Turn ON the second toggle: Email link (passwordless sign-in).
  4. Click Save.
3 Confirm Google Sign-In Provider
Active
  1. In the Sign-in method tab, check that Google is listed and Enabled.
  2. Ensure a Project support email is selected.
  3. Click Save if making any updates.
4 Add Authorized Domains
Security
Firebase will reject OTP requests if the origin domain is not whitelisted.
  1. In Authentication, click the Settings tab (next to Sign-in method).
  2. Click on Authorized domains in the sub-menu.
  3. Verify and add the following domains:
• localhost (local development)
• visvam.in (production domain)
• visvam-harvest.vercel.app (or any active preview / staging URLs)
