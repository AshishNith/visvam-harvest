## B. Cookie Notice (dedicated page)

_Last updated: [DD MONTH 2026]_

This Cookie Notice explains how Viśvam (a brand of Tej Kripa Private Limited) uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy].

### What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your choices, and — where you allow it — help us understand usage and measure advertising. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and tags, including the Meta Pixel.

### The cookies we use

**Strictly necessary cookies**
These keep the site working — enabling navigation, cart, checkout, and security. The site cannot function properly without them, so they do not require consent and cannot be switched off.

**Analytics cookies**
These help us understand how visitors use the site — which pages are viewed and how people move through the store — so we can improve it. [Provider: Google Analytics / Shopify analytics.] These load only if you consent.

**Advertising cookies**
These power the **Meta Pixel** and related Meta advertising tools. They let us measure how our Facebook and Instagram ads perform, understand which products interest visitors, and show relevant Viśvam ads to you and people like you. They may share information such as pages viewed, actions taken, and cookie or device identifiers with Meta. These load only if you consent. See §8 of our Privacy Policy for detail.

### Your choices

- On your first visit, our cookie banner lets you **accept all**, **reject non-essential**, or **choose by category**.
- You can change your choice at any time using the **"Cookie settings"** link in our footer.
- You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.
- For Meta ads specifically, you can adjust your ad preferences within your Facebook and Instagram account settings.

Declining analytics or advertising cookies will not affect your ability to browse or place an order.

### Notes for you (not for the site)
- The banner promise "nothing non-essential loads before consent" is a real obligation, not just copy. Configure your Shopify **Customer Privacy / consent settings** so the Meta Pixel and analytics fire **only after** consent — Shopify's built-in consent banner or an app like a consent-mode tool handles this. If the pixel fires before consent, the notice is inaccurate.
- Delete whichever analytics provider you don't actually use.
- Add the "Cookie settings" footer link so people can genuinely change their minds — the notice references it.
